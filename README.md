## 🎮 Welcome to PONG!

Get ready to experience the legendary arcade game **PONG**, recreated as an interactive browser game!

Challenge yourself, keep the ball alive, beat your personal records, and become the ultimate Pong champion.

No installation. No complicated setup. Just open your browser and play!

## ✨ Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| ▶️ Start | Begin a new game |
| ⏸️ Pause | Pause your current game |
| ⏹️ End | End the current session |
| 🔄 Restart | Start again from the beginning |
| 🏆 Personal Records | Track your best ball-hit achievements and winning records |
| ⚙️ Settings | Explore additional game settings and options |
| 📊 Game Statistics | View your gaming performance |
| 🕹️ Latest Games | Check your recent matches and results |

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Open `pong.html` in your browser.
2. Press **START** to begin playing.
3. Control your paddle and keep the ball in play.
4. Beat your previous highest hit record.
5. Win games and improve your statistics.
6. Explore the settings and check your latest game history.

**Your mission:** Beat your own records and become a Pong master! 🏆

## 🛠️ Built With

- **HTML5** — Game structure and layout.
- **CSS3** — Styling, animations, and visual design.
- **JavaScript** — Game mechanics, controls, and statistics.
